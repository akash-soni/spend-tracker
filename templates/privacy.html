Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and notes you enter while using the app.
  • Usage data: basic logs such as login timestamps, used solely for security and debugging.

We do not collect payment information, government identifiers, or any data beyond what is necessary to provide the service.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Provide and maintain your personal expense tracking experience.
  • Authenticate your identity and keep your account secure.
  • Generate summaries and reports visible only to you.
  • Diagnose and fix technical issues with the service.

We will never use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to delivering the service.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

You may delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all associated personal data and expense records will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We may use limited third-party infrastructure (such as hosting providers) to operate the service; these providers are contractually bound to handle your data only as directed by us.

This policy does not cover external websites that may be linked from within the app. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our website.
